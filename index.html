What I'm curious about

I haven't picked a single lane yet, and for now I'm happy about that. These are the four directions that keep pulling me in.

⛨

Security

What fascinates me is how almost everything around us can turn into a vulnerability. Some of the things we use every day are unsafe only because "that's how it's always been done." I like finding the gap between how something is supposed to work and how it actually does.

⚙

Systems

Kernels and concurrency taught me how the machine really works underneath. What I enjoy most is using that understanding to squeeze time out of something, or to see how it breaks, which is where systems meet security. Honestly, it's also just fun to know how it all fits together.

λ

Compilers

Compilers was one of my favourite classes: watching plain text turn into structure, then meaning, then machine code. I'd like to explore where compilers meet security and blockchain, such as smart-contract languages, bytecode, and the bugs that slip through translation.

◉

AI

I'm taking a Deep Learning class this semester and it has completely hooked me. It's an exciting field to be learning while it's still taking shape. Before that I came at AI through constraint solving, data mining techniques and through data automation at EY.
